Palantir v City Hall

“Objective criteria”, measured

Palantir took London's policing office to the High Court after it blocked a proposed Met Police contract, then settled in October 2026. Palantir says public money should be spent on objective criteria, on a level playing field. Here is what happened, and Palantir's UK record measured against that principle.

What happened

    Palantir's record, against its own test

    City Hall's stated grounds, as reported, were procurement rules and value for money: a pilot was to become a two-year contract without a competition. The settlement records that the decision was not based on Palantir's values or ethics, and the Met is now running an open procurement that Palantir can bid in.
