The directors of Palantir Technologies UK, Ltd. (together with its branch, the "Company") present their strategic report on the
affairs of the Company, together with the directors’ report, financial statements and auditor's report, for the year ended
31 December 2022.

The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir International Inc., which is in tum a wholly owned subsidiary of
Palantir Technologies Inc., the ultimate parent in Palantir’s global group of companies (the “Ultimate Parent Company”). The
Company has a branch office in the Netherlands and subsidiaries in the United Kingdom (the “UK”) and Qatar.

Principal Activity

The principal activity of the Company during the 2022 year was that of the provision of marketing and sales support services,
developmental support, technical implementation, and business support services, including certain contracting services, to the
Ultimate Parent Company. The Ultimate Parent Company builds software that empowers organisations to effectively integrate
their data, decisions, and operations at scale.

The compensation received by the Company for the services listed above, to the Ultimate Parent Company, is calculated as
attributable costs plus a mark-up percentage of service fee that is being periodically reviewed and adjusted as deemed
appropriate to maintain an arm’s-length rate of compensation. For the year 2022, the service fee percentage amounted to 7%
(2021: 5%).

Business Review

In the 2022 year, the Company achieved turnover of £236,288,274 (2021: £185,908,560), an increase of £50,379,714 over the
previous year primarily due to increased intercompany revenue, which is 7% (2021: 5%) mark-up of operating expenses
excluding unrealised FX gains and losses. Operating expenses for the year amounted to £216,341,228 (2021: £174,1 19,340), an
increase of £42,221,888 over the previous year. The increase in 2022 operating expense was primarily due to increase in wages
and staff related expenses. Operating profit before taxation amounted to £19,947,944 (2021: £11,796,740), an increase of
£8,151,204 primarily due to an increase in turnover and an increase in unrealised gain from foreign currency exchange in 2022
as compared to an unrealised loss in 2021.

A significant portion of the Company revenue comes from the Ultimate Parent Company, and accordingly the performance of
the Company is closely linked to that of the Ultimate Parent Company. The strategic objectives and future prospects of the
Company are, therefore, aligned with the Ultimate Parent Company. Further detail can be found in the Ultimate Parent
Company’s publicly available filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), including in the Ultimate
Parent Company’s most recent Annual Report on Form 10-K, Proxy Statement on Schedule 14A, and other periodic reports of
the Ultimate Parent Company, as may be amended or supplemented from time to time, none of which form part of this report.
The Company is included in the consolidated financial accounts of the Ultimate Parent Company, which are available to the
public and accessible on the investor relations website (https://investors.palantir.com/financials).

Key Performance Indicators (“KPIs”)

Because most turnover arises from the cost-plus arrangement with the Ultimate Parent Company, the Company’s directors are of
the opinion that analysis using KPIs is not necessary for an understanding of the development, performance or position of the
business.
