PALANTIR TECHNOLOGIES UK, LTD.
STRATEGIC REPORT

FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
REGISTERED NO. 07042994

The directors of Palantir Technologies UK, Ltd. (together with its branch, the “Company”) present their strategic report on the
affairs of the Company, together with the directors’ report, financial statements and auditor's report, for the year ended
31 December 2025.

The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir International Inc., which is in turn a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir
Technologies Inc. (the “Ultimate Parent Company”), the ultimate parent in Palantir’s global group of companies (collectively,
the “Group” or “Palantir”). The Company has a branch office in the Netherlands and subsidiaries in the United Kingdom (the
“UK”) and Qatar.

Principal Activity

The principal activity of the Company during the 2025 year was that of the provision of marketing and sales support services,
developmental support, technical implementation, and business support services, including certain contracting services, to the
Ultimate Parent Company as well as sales directly to customers based within and outside of the United Kingdom. The Ultimate
Parent Company builds software that empowers organisations to effectively integrate their data, decisions, and operations at
scale,

The compensation received by the Company for the services to the Ultimate Parent Company listed above is calculated as
attributable costs plus a mark-up percentage of service fee that is periodically reviewed and adjusted as deemed appropriate to
maintain an arm’s-length rate of compensation. For the year 2025, the service fee percentage amounted to 7% (2024: 7%).

Business Review

In 2025, the Company achieved turnover of £262,538,425 (2024: £247,298,448), an increase of £15,239,977 over the previous
year primarily due to increased activity. Operating expenses for the year amounted to £238,173,327 (2024: £223,061,169), an
increase of £15,112,158 over the previous year. The increase in 2025 operating expenses was primarily due to increases in
wages & staff related expenses and research & development expenses. Profit before taxation amounted to £25,133,656 (2024:
£25,314,204), a decrease of £180,548 primarily due to a decrease in interest receivable and similar income.

‘The Company's statement of financial position as at 31 December 2025 is on page 19.

A significant portion of the Company's revenue comes from the Ultimate Parent Company, and accordingly the performance of
the Company is closely linked to that of the Ultimate Parent Company. The strategic objectives and future prospects of the
Company are, therefore, aligned with the Ultimate Parent Company. Further detail can be found in the Ultimate Parent
Company’s publicly available filings with the United States (“US”) Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), including in
the Ultimate Parent Company’s most recent Annual Report on Form 10-K, Proxy Statement on Schedule 14A, and other
periodic reports of the Ultimate Parent Company, as may be amended or supplemented from time to time, none of which form
part of this report.

The Company's results are included in the consolidated financial statements of the Ultimate Parent Company, which are
available to the public and accessible on the SEC website (https://www.sec.gov).

Key Performance Indicators (“KPIs”)

The Group manages its business and measures the delivery of its strategic objectives through the application of KPIs at operating
business and group level, details of which can be found in the Ultimate Parent Company’s publicly available SEC filings. As the
Company continues to operate under a cost-plus arrangement with the Ultimate Parent Company, the Company’s directors are of
the opinion that analysis using KPIs is not necessary for an understanding of the development, performance or position of the
business.
