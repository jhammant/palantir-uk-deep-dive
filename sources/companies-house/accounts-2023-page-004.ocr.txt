The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir International Inc.. which is in turn a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir
Technologies Inc., the ultimate parent in Palantir’s global group of companies (the “Ultimate Parent Company” or “Ultimate
Parent"). The Company has a branch office in the Netherlands and subsidiaries in the United Kingdom (the “UK™) and Qatar.
Principal Activity
The principal activity of the Company during the 2023 year was that of the provision of marketing and sales support services,
dev clopmental support, technical implementation. and business support services. including certain contracting services, to the
Ultimate Parent Company. The Ultimate Parent Company builds software that empowers organisations to effectively integrate
their data. decisions. and operations at scale.
The compensation received by the Company for the services listed above. to the Ultimate Parent Company, is calculated as
attributable costs plus a mark-up percentage of service fee that is being periodically reviewed and adjusted as deemed
appropriate to maintain an arm’s-length rate of compensation. For the year 2023, the service fee percentage amounted to 7%
(2022: 7%).
Business Review
In the 2023 year, the Company achieved turnover of £232.772.182 (2022: £236.288,274). a decrease of £3.516,092 over the
previous year primarily due to decreased intercompany revenue, which is 7% (2022: 7%) mark-up of operating expenses
excluding unrealised FX gains and losses. Operating expenses for the year amounted to £214.619.970 (2022: £216,341,228). a
decrease of £1.721.258 over the previous year. The decrease in 2023 operating expense was primarily due to decreases in share-
based payments and legal and professional fees. Operating profit before taxation amounted to £19.117.779 (2022: £19.947.944).
a decrease of £830,165 primarily duc to a decrease in turnover and an inereasc in unrealised loss from foreign currency exchange
in 2023 as compared to an unrealised gain from currency exchange in 2022.
A significant portion of the Company revenue comes from the Ultimate Parent Company, and accordingly the performance of
the Company is closely linked to that of the Ultimate Parent Company. The strategic objectives and future prospects of the
Company are, therefore. aligned with the Ultimate Parent Company. Further detail can be found in the Ultimate Parent
Company's publicly available filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), including in the Ultimate
Parent Companys most recent Annual Report on Form 10-K. Proxy Statement on Schedule 144, and other periodic reports of
the Ultimate Parent Company. as may be amended or supplemented from time to time, none of which form part of this report.
The Company's results are included in the consolidated financial statements of the Ultimate Parent Company. which are
available to the public and accessible on the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission website (https://www.sec.gov).
